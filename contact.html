Showroom Assistance

Frequently Inquired

What are the lead times for custom bespoke commissions? ▼

In-stock collection items dispatch within 48 to 72 hours via our white-glove transport team. Custom bespoke commissions (custom dimensions, rare woods, or specialized leathers) typically require 4 to 6 weeks for precision atelier fabrication and quality inspection.

What does Praise Furniture White-Glove Delivery entail? ▼

Our white-glove service includes insured transit in temperature-controlled vehicles, scheduled appointment windows, placement in your designated room of choice, professional hardware assembly, leveling, and complete removal of all protective crating and packaging materials.

Can I request textile and wood finish swatches prior to ordering? ▼

Yes. We offer complimentary curated material sample boxes containing Belgian bouclé swatches, full-grain Italian leather samples, and timber finish blocks shipped directly to your residence or design studio.

What warranty coverage is included with Praise Furniture acquisitions? ▼

Every piece crafted by Praise Furniture carries our comprehensive 10-Year Structural Guarantee covering internal solid hardwood joinery, framing, and steel reinforcements.
